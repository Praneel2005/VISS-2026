Day 1 Tasks

Task 1: Image Transforms
Both images above started as the exact same checkerboard grid and were rotated by 45 degrees. Which image used Forward Mapping and which used Backward Mapping? Why do the black holes appear in one, and what specific interpolation method caused the jagged lines in the other?

Task 2: Perspective Projection
Two identical cars are parked on a straight road. Car A is 10 meters away from your camera, and Car B is 20 meters away. In your final image, exactly how does the width of Car B (in pixels) compare to Car A? State the mathematical reason.

Task 3: Camera Matrices
You mount your camera on a drone, fly it 50 meters into the air, and tilt it downwards to take a photo. In the camera projection matrix P = K[R|t], did this action change the Intrinsic matrix (K), the Extrinsic matrix ([R|t]), or both? Explain why.

Task 4: Focal Length
Without moving your camera's physical position, you swap the lens, effectively doubling the focal length. Does your Field of View (FOV) increase or decrease? What happens to the apparent size of the objects in the image?
